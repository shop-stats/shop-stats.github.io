Daily intake per person

Your daily calorie need

With one person on the card, the calorie target comes from your details: your basal metabolic rate (BMR, the energy you burn at rest, using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation) multiplied by how active you are. Your details are saved only in this browser.

Average daily calories per two-week stretch

Calories bought in each fortnight, divided by 14 days and the number of people. Food bought in one fortnight is often eaten in the next, so single stretches swing more than real eating does.
